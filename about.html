OUR STORY

Welcome to the Club

The APU Pickleball Club was created to introduce this exciting hybrid sport to students and staff in a fun and inclusive way. Our club is a place to make new friends, stay active and enjoy friendly matches.

We focus on creating a welcoming environment where everyone can learn, improve and enjoy pickleball together, regardless of skill level.

Join the Club

WHAT IS PICKLEBALL?

Fast, Fun and Beginner-Friendly

Pickleball is a racket sport that combines elements of badminton, tennis and table tennis. It is played on a smaller court using a solid paddle and a perforated plastic ball.

Because of the smaller court size and simple rules, pickleball is easy to learn and suitable for players of all ages and fitness levels.

  • Simple rules – you can learn in one session.
  • Short games – perfect between classes.
  • Low impact – friendly for all fitness levels.

WHY PICKLEBALL AT APU?

A Sport that Builds Community

At APU, pickleball helps students build teamwork, communication and sportsmanship. The game is social, fast-paced and suitable for casual play as well as more competitive events.

The club also supports a healthy campus lifestyle by giving students a fun way to exercise between classes and assessments.

Teamwork Friendship Campus Life

WHO CAN JOIN?

Open to All APU Students & Staff

The APU Pickleball Club welcomes complete beginners, casual players and experienced athletes. No prior racket sports experience is required.

Just bring your sports attire and enthusiasm. Basic equipment can be provided during training sessions for new members.
