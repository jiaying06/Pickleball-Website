PLAY FAIR · STAY SAFE

Club Rules & Court Etiquette

To make sure every training session and match is safe, fair and enjoyable, members are expected to follow these rules and guidelines when joining APU Pickleball Club activities.

Court Safety Fair Play Respect & Fun

General Conduct

  • Respect all players, coaches, committee members and venue staff.
  • Arrive on time for training sessions, matches and club events.
  • No abusive language, bullying or unsportsmanlike behaviour.
  • Follow instructions given by the club committee and game officials.

Safety Rules

  • Always warm up properly before playing to prevent injury.
  • Do not swing paddles recklessly near other players.
  • Stop play immediately if someone is injured or the court is unsafe.
  • Report any accidents or hazards to the committee members.

Equipment & Attire

  • Wear proper sports attire and non-marking court shoes.
  • Use pickleball paddles and balls approved by the club or coach.
  • Label personal equipment clearly with your name.
  • Handle club equipment with care and return it after use.

Basic Playing Rules

  1. The serve must be made diagonally, starting from the right service area.
  2. Only the serving side can score points.
  3. The ball must bounce once on each side after the serve (double-bounce rule).
  4. No volleying is allowed inside the non-volley zone (“the kitchen”).
  5. Games are usually played to 11 or 15 points, win by 2.
