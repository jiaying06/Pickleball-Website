Equipment & Attire

This page outlines the required and recommended equipment and attire for club members.

Club Jersey

Official jersey worn during events and competitions.

Required

Sports Shoes

Proper footwear to prevent injuries during activities.

Required

Water Bottle

Recommended to stay hydrated during training sessions.

Optional

Attire Guidelines

Do ✅

  • Wear club jersey during official events
  • Wear comfortable sports attire
  • Use proper sports shoes

Don't ❌

  • Wear slippers or sandals
  • Wear offensive or inappropriate clothing
  • Ignore safety equipment
