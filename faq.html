Need Help?

Find quick answers to common questions about our club, membership, training sessions and events.

Membership

All APU students are welcome to join, regardless of skill level. Beginners are encouraged.

You can register via the Membership Registration page.

Membership fee details will be announced by the club committee if applicable.

Training & Skill Levels

No experience is required. Training sessions are suitable for all levels.

Wear comfortable sports attire. Equipment may be provided during sessions.

Events & Tournaments

Register through the Event Registration page when events are open.

Some events may be open to non-members. Please refer to the event details.

Support & Enquiries

Visit our Contact Us page or submit an enquiry form.

You can submit feedback via the Feedback Form.
