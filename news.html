Announcement

APU Pickleball Club Official Launch

10 March 2025 · APU Sports Hall

The APU Pickleball Club has officially launched with an introductory session at the APU Sports Hall. New members experienced basic drills, game rules and friendly matches guided by our student coaches.

Training

Weekly Training Sessions

Every Wednesday & Friday · 6.00 PM – 8.00 PM

Regular training sessions are held twice a week. Beginners and experienced players are welcome. Equipment can be provided for new members, so you only need to bring sports attire, a water bottle and good energy.

Tournament

Upcoming Internal Tournament

Coming Soon · Mixed Doubles & Singles

The club is planning an internal tournament with singles and doubles categories. Brackets, prizes and registration details will be announced through email and social media – stay tuned!
